# My Favorite Movie  
![Movie Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/7/7a/1917poster.jpg)

## Movie Title: *1917*  
**Genre:** War / Drama  
**Director:** Sam Mendes  

---

### 🎬 Why I Like This Movie  
- It has an amazing one-shot camera style.  
- The story is emotional and realistic.  
- The cinematography and music are outstanding.

### 📘 Summary  
*1917* is one of the best war movies I’ve seen. It captures the emotion, fear, and bravery of soldiers during World War I. The movie shows how courage and determination can overcome even the toughest challenges.
